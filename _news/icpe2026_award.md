Our paper "[Evaluating Kubernetes Performance for GenAI Inference: From Automatic Speech Recognition to LLM Summarization](https://doi.org/10.1145/3777884.3796983)" received the Best Industry Paper Award at the 17th ACM/SPEC International Conference on Performance Engineering ([ICPE 2026](https://icpe2026.spec.org)) in Florence, Italy. This is our second Best Industry Paper Award at ICPE, after our work on production-ready autoscaling at ICPE 2022.
